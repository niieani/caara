---
name: delegated-grilling
description: Spawn a delegated architecture decision partner. Use when the user wants to delegate the grilling process to a subagent.
---

When grilling, spawn a persistent subagent in-lieu of me, explain the context of the problem, and instead of asking me the questions directly, ask the subagent. In other words - do the back and forth with the subagent, instead of me (the user). I delegate decision power to it. When initially prompting the subagent, ask it to act in the best interest of the project long term, based on its understanding of the project, and to consider the question carefully, rather than simply always accepting the suggestion. Once grilling is finished, and any ADRs written, domain language updated, present a short summary of the decisions made.
