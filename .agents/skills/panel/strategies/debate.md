# Debate

Dominant risk: **contested direction** — a judgment call between competing approaches, resolved by
committed positions arguing under evidence rather than one model's first instinct.

## Seats

| Seat | Count | Mode | Family rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| debater | 2–3 | cold-blind for the opening position, warm every round after | distinct families |
| fresh juror | on stall | cold-briefed | a family not yet in the exchange, roster permitting |
| synthesis | 1 | cold-briefed | per the seating tests |

Debaters stay warm across rounds because a debater must own its prior commitments to concede with
cause coherently — that accumulated bias is the feature. Restarting a debater discards its
commitments; do it only if its thread is lost.

## Flow

1. **Opening positions.** Same framing to every debater, cold-blind, in parallel. Each writes a
   position artifact stating its recommendation and its explicit concede-condition ("I would
   concede if X") — the [posture block](../panelist-contract.md) is mandatory here and in every
   later round.
2. **Rounds.** Forward to each debater (warm) the artifact *paths* of every other debater's latest
   round as required reading. Each reads in full, then defends or concedes-with-cause in a new
   round artifact. Your forwarding prompt is neutral: paths only, no framing of who is ahead.
3. **Check movement after every round** with the stall test (SKILL.md step 6). Also probe any
   debater that fully agrees within the first two rounds — early consensus is the suspicious
   outcome, not the goal.
4. **Converge or break.** Consensus signal: every debater's latest artifact states the same
   decision and no stated concede-condition remains untested. Stalls follow SKILL.md step 6 (fresh
   juror, then forced synthesis); a juror ruling either produces a concession-with-cause or the
   point survives to synthesis as dissent.
5. **Synthesize and adjudicate** per the contract and SKILL.md step 6.

## Convergence point

Consensus signal, or forced synthesis after two stalled rounds.
